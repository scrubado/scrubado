Quick answers

Questions? We've got the basics.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Scrubado does not require a paid cleaning membership to book a cleaning.

Where does Scrubado operate?

Scrubado's initial service area is Rhode Island, excluding ZIP code 02807 (New Shoreham / Block Island). Availability also depends on cleaners accepting jobs in your area.

Are the cleaners Scrubado employees?

Scrubado is designed as a marketplace that connects customers with independent cleaners.

How much does a cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the service, details, and selected add-ons. The booking system calculates your quote; we don't advertise made-up flat rates.

Can I book recurring cleanings?

The planned booking options include one-time, weekly (12% off), and biweekly (8% off) cleanings, subject to availability and final quote rules.
